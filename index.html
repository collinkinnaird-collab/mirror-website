Real-time form coaching

Prop up your phone, step into frame, and Mirror gives you a grade and a tip after every rep — without anyone watching.

Goals that ladder

Set a North-Star goal like "500 push-ups this month" and Mirror suggests stepping-stone weekly goals that build toward it.

Honest about what it is

Mirror's coach is a rule-based engine, not magic AI. Every score and tip comes from form rules we wrote and you can read about. The honesty is the moat.

Your data, your phone

Mirror has no servers. Your workouts stay on your device and (if you're signed in) in your private iCloud. We never see them.

Apple Watch companion

Live rep count on your wrist while you work out, with a haptic on every rep and a tap-to-end button.

Trophies and streaks

Show up, level up. Mirror tracks streaks, awards trophies for milestones, and gives you a weekly recap so progress feels earned.
